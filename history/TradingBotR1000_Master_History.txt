1. ORIGIN AND PURPOSE OF THE PROJECT
------------------------------------
TradingBotR1000 is an automated trading system developed in Python, initially with the assistance
of OpenAI Codex in a local Windows environment.

The project was created to transform a rule-based quantitative trading strategy into a system
capable of autonomously handling the operational cycle: market-data preparation and validation,
signal identification, position sizing, order planning and transmission, execution monitoring,
reconciliation, health monitoring and reporting.

Development and early testing began locally on Windows. The system was later transferred to a
Hetzner Ubuntu/Linux server, which is now the primary operational environment. The architectural
goal is that the bot can operate continuously without depending on the user's personal PC being
powered on.

TradingBotR1000 currently operates exclusively in Interactive Brokers (IBKR) PAPER trading. The
paper phase is intended to validate not only the strategy but the complete operational system:
IBKR connectivity, scheduling, data availability, sizing, order transmission, failure handling,
restart behaviour, reconciliation, reporting and remote control.

The required duration of the paper-trading validation period has not yet been finally defined.
Any future transition to LIVE trading must require explicit validation and authorization and must
not occur automatically merely because development is complete.


2. ORIGIN OF THE TRADING STRATEGY
---------------------------------
The strategy is a long-only mean-reversion strategy for US equities. It was reconstructed from a
transcript of a Rayner Teo educational video supplied as a project source.

The core concept is to buy temporary weakness in securities that remain in a longer-term uptrend,
seeking to capture the subsequent reversion or bounce. The source strategy explicitly buys the
pullback without waiting for a reversal-confirmation pattern.

The source strategy uses Russell 1000 stocks on a daily timeframe, with up to five simultaneous
positions and a 20% capital allocation per position, without leverage.

Source retained in the ChatGPT project history: uploaded transcript supplied on 2026-08-28.


3. TRADING RULES CURRENTLY IMPLEMENTED
--------------------------------------
The following rules were verified against the code actually running on the Hetzner server on
2026-08-28.

- Universe: Russell 1000 stocks.
- Timeframe: daily.
- Direction: long-only.
- Configured strategic investable capital: 100% of Net Liquidation Value.
- Strategic liquidity reserve: 0%.
- Position allocation: 20% of investable capital per position.
- Maximum simultaneous positions: 5.
- Leverage: not allowed by the strategy.
- Trend filter: closing price above the 200-day simple moving average.
- Pullback condition: closing price below the lower 20-day Bollinger Band using 2.5 standard
  deviations.
- Entry price: buy limit at 97% of the signal-day close (3% below that close).
- Ranking: if valid candidates exceed available position slots, rank by price appreciation over
  the previous 150 days and prefer the strongest candidates.
- Exit indicator: 2-period RSI.
- RSI exit threshold currently coded: cross above 50.
- Time exit: maximum holding period of 10 trading days.

The source video also states that the daily RSI signal is only confirmed when the market closes,
so the associated exit is acted on at the next market opening rather than after-hours.

IMPORTANT OPEN QUESTION - RSI 50 VS RSI 60:
The user remembers that the historical backtest may have changed the exit threshold from RSI 50
to RSI 60. However, on 2026-08-28 both the production code and its tests used RSI 50, which is also
the value in the original video strategy. The RSI=60 recollection must therefore not be treated as
confirmed history until the original backtest results or earlier project evidence are recovered.
The production parameter must not be changed on memory alone.


4. BACKTEST AND CHANGES FROM THE SOURCE STRATEGY
------------------------------------------------
A historical backtest was conducted using approximately ten years of market data downloaded from
Massive. The purpose was to validate and, where justified, refine the source strategy before and
during development of the automated system.

The user recalls that the principal, and possibly only, strategy-level change produced by the
backtest was an RSI exit threshold of 60 rather than 50. This remains UNVERIFIED for the reason
recorded in Section 3.

Until the original backtest reports, scripts and/or historical development records are reviewed,
this file intentionally does not invent or reconstruct backtest conclusions that cannot be proven.


5. CAPITAL PROTECTION AND OPERATIONAL SIZING
--------------------------------------------
An important distinction was introduced during development between strategic investable capital
and cash that IBKR can actually accept for new orders.

The strategy is configured to allow 100% of capital to be investable, but this does not authorize
the bot to submit aggregate new orders exceeding the funds actually spendable at the broker.

On 2026-08-28 the order-sizing logic was corrected so that the operational buy budget is capped by
the minimum of:

- configured/effective investable capital;
- cash;
- Available Funds;
- Buying Power.

This preserves the 100%-investable strategy while preventing planned orders from being sized above
currently spendable funds. The correction was validated by the relevant automated test suite,
which passed 55/55 applicable operational tests before the controller was restarted with the new
code.

7. TELEGRAM REMOTE INTERFACE
----------------------------
Telegram is integrated as a remote monitoring and operational-control channel.

Its purpose extends beyond trade notifications: from a mobile device the operator should be able
to inspect system status, IBKR status, portfolio/account information and relevant operational
results without opening an SSH session.

The project has progressively introduced or improved Telegram reporting for areas including:
- system status and heartbeat;
- IBKR connectivity/disconnect events;
- portfolio/account summary;
- investable capital mode and amount;
- scan completion;
- planned/submitted/rejected or skipped orders;
- operational health information.

A controlled IB Gateway restart mechanism has also been developed for Telegram. Sensitive actions
are intended to require stronger controls than read-only commands; the gateway-restart design uses
explicit confirmation and a short confirmation expiry window rather than an unrestricted remote
shell command.

Telegram credentials and tokens are secrets and must remain outside this history and outside Git.

10. SSH, SERVER ACCESS AND AUTHORIZATION MODEL
----------------------------------------------
Traditional administrative access to the server is through SSH using the Linux account ibkradmin.

The project deliberately separates normal bot operation from server-administration privileges.
The objective is to avoid granting root-level power to an automated component when a narrower
permission is sufficient.

The same least-privilege principle is applied to remote AI access. Operational automation should
be powerful enough to maintain the project, but access to unrelated home-directory content,
credentials, operating-system administration and root actions should remain separately controlled.


11. SENTINELX INTRODUCTION
--------------------------
SentinelX was introduced in August 2026 as a controlled bridge between ChatGPT and the Hetzner
server.

The immediate problem it addresses is the previous manual workflow:
ChatGPT -> copy command -> SSH terminal -> execute -> copy output -> paste output into ChatGPT.

With SentinelX, ChatGPT can directly execute permitted server operations, inspect permitted files
and services, read the results and, where explicitly authorized, edit project files.

Security choices made during installation/configuration include:
- SentinelX runs as its own Linux service account named sentinelx;
- it was installed without general passwordless sudo (SENTINELX_SKIP_SUDO=1);
- the project path was explicitly granted read/write access in SentinelX policy;
- Linux ACLs were added so the sentinelx account can traverse the necessary parent path and read/
  write the TradingBotR1000 project without receiving unrestricted access to the entire ibkradmin
  home directory;
- ChatGPT was subsequently configured not to request repeated user confirmation for every SentinelX
  operation, while the server-side SentinelX policy and Linux permissions remain the actual access
  boundaries.

SentinelX is not itself the final AI Operator. It is the secure server execution/communication layer
on which a more general AI-assisted operating system for this and future projects can be built.


12. PC + MOBILE OPERATIONS AND THE FUTURE AI OPERATOR
------------------------------------------------------
A core project requirement is that operation and maintenance must not depend on a single Windows PC.

The system must progressively be operable from both PC and mobile devices. This includes monitoring,
alerting, portfolio/order inspection, diagnosis and, within controlled permissions, maintenance and
recovery actions.

Telegram is one mobile operational interface. SentinelX provides remote technical access from
ChatGPT to the Linux server. The planned AI Operator should combine these concepts into a simpler,
user-friendly interface in which the user can also paste text and images while the agent handles the
underlying server interactions rather than requiring manual command copying.

The AI Operator must not be hard-wired to TradingBotR1000. It should be reusable if the user creates
a second trading bot, changes trading strategy, or operates another server-side project.

The intended separation is therefore:

TradingBotR1000
    = one specific automated trading project and strategy

AI Operator
    = reusable management/maintenance layer able to operate TradingBotR1000 and future projects

A further requirement is durable project memory independent of the user's PC. This Master History
TXT file is the first human-readable component of that memory. The future agent should maintain it
automatically so that the reasoning and sequence by which changes were conceived, tested, accepted,
rejected or corrected are not lost.

[2026-09-04] BACKTEST CAPACITY METHODOLOGY — TWO-STAGE APPROACH
Follow-up decision on the capacity problem of the current concentrated mean-reversion strategy. The backtest should NOT attempt from daily OHLC data alone to claim an exact maximum deployable capital, because Low <= 97% only proves that the limit price was reached; it does not prove that enough shares were available at that price to fill a large order, nor reproduce queue priority or the market impact of a real order.

Agreed methodology: use a two-stage process. Stage 1 is the long-horizon daily backtest, whose purpose is to establish whether the strategy has a sufficiently robust theoretical edge and deserves further investigation. Capacity analysis is deferred until the strategy passes that stage, avoiding unnecessary complexity and data cost.

Stage 2, if justified, is a more granular execution/capacity validation using intraday data. For each theoretical 97% fill, one potentially useful test is to examine the volume actually traded at/around the limit price and during the relevant interval, rather than treating every price touch as a guaranteed full fill. The analysis can then consider order size relative to available/traded volume, partial fills, spread/slippage and plausible market impact. This is recognized as substantially more complex than the primary backtest and therefore should not be imposed on the initial long-horizon study.

The capacity result should preferably be expressed as a capacity curve or range — estimated executability/performance as capital and order size increase (for example across several capital levels) — rather than as a falsely precise single maximum-capital number. If necessary, a later empirical calibration could compare model/PAPER expectations with carefully controlled small real executions, but this is a separate validation phase and not required for the initial backtest.

USER DECISION: proceed with this staged methodology. Specifically remember the possibility of checking traded volumes for each theoretical 97% fill, while treating that check as a second-level validation because of its complexity.
